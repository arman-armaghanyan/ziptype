Ziptype for Mac · Preview

Try version 1.0.6 (7) for Apple Silicon and Intel Macs running macOS 13 or later. The app and installer are Developer ID signed and Apple notarized. This is an early preview, not a stable release.

Before you download: text expansion in real applications is still being verified. You may encounter issues with expansion or custom editors. Google sign-in and cloud sync remain limited to configured test accounts. Local library use does not require an account. Export a backup before updating and start with non-sensitive test shortcuts.

Installation and preview notes · Verify the download checksum

  1. Quit an existing copy of Ziptype, then open the DMG and drag Ziptype.app into Applications. Replace only the app, not your saved library.
  2. Open Ziptype from Applications. It stays available in the menu bar.
  3. To try text expansion in other apps, enable Accessibility for that installed copy in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility. Library access works without this permission.
  4. Try a disposable shortcut in a standard text field. If it does not expand, report the app, field, and Ziptype version to support.

If macOS rejects the installer, stop and contact support. Do not bypass Gatekeeper or delete your library to get past a warning. Notarization does not certify that every feature works or that updates cannot lose data.

Before updating or syncing

Export a backup of your shortcuts first. Signing in does not always mean your library has uploaded. Check the sync status on the device containing the library you want to keep, and review the device and cloud versions before choosing either one.

Keep your existing extension installed. Removing it can remove its local data.

Prompt library

Copy a preset or group from the prompt library. Open its “How to use” guide for the instructions supported by your app version.

Privacy and terms

The published Privacy Policy describes the earlier local-only extension and needs updating for account and cloud-sync features before their public release. The Terms of Use are still a draft for review.
