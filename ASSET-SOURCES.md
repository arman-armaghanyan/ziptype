# Self-hosted assets

- Geist Sans variable, Geist Mono variable, Geist Pixel Square: official
  `geist@1.7.2` npm distribution from Vercel. Original binaries, SIL Open Font
  License 1.1, included in `fonts/LICENSE.txt`.
  Source: https://vercel.com/font and https://github.com/vercel/geist-font.
- Application icons: `@lobehub/icons-static-svg@1.95.0`, original SVGs.
  MIT license included in `assets/apps/LICENSE.txt`.
  Source: https://github.com/lobehub/lobe-icons.
  The underlying brand marks belong to their respective owners. Their use
  identifies example workflows, not endorsement or certified compatibility.
  Includes ChatGPT/OpenAI, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, Copilot, Cursor, Notion,
  Midjourney, FLUX, Runway, Kling, Suno, ElevenLabs, Poe, OpenRouter, Figma,
  and Recraft. Ideogram's previous LobeHub mark has been replaced below.
- Picsart: original public SVG favicon linked by [Picsart's website](https://picsart.com/),
  retrieved September 18, 2026 from its official CDN:
  https://cdn-cms-uploads.picsart.com/cms-uploads/8cf1cbff-3ab9-4b27-b012-688bbbb181e5.svg.
- OpenArt: transparent mark extracted from the official vector logo linked by
  [OpenArt's creation page](https://openart.ai/create), retrieved September 18, 2026:
  https://cdn.openart.ai/assets/home/openart_logo_20240529.svg.
  Its mark path is unchanged, with the wordmark omitted and white fill changed
  to near-black for the light icon tile. The former `openart.png` favicon remains
  in the asset folder but is no longer referenced or counted in page payloads.
- Ideogram: updated mark extracted from the official [Ideogram homepage](https://ideogram.ai/)
  logo on September 18, 2026: https://ideogram.ai/_astro/new-logo.wmOvXw7A.svg.
  The two brain-symbol subpaths and official dark-purple fill are unchanged;
  the wordmark paths are omitted and the viewBox fits the symbol.
  These official assets are brand identifiers, not MIT-licensed LobeHub assets;
  their marks remain the property of the respective companies. No endorsement
  or certified integration is implied.
- Gmail and Google Docs: lightweight, locally drawn SVG representations used
  only to identify the illustrative application previews. Brand marks remain
  the property of Google; these are not embedded applications or endorsements.
- The hero's Ziptype logo path is reused from the existing extension popup.
- App categories were checked against the public pages for
  [Poe](https://poe.com/), [OpenRouter](https://openrouter.ai/),
  [Runway](https://runway.com/), and [ElevenLabs](https://elevenlabs.io/).
  These sources describe the tools, not evidence of tested Ziptype compatibility.
- Favicon: existing Ziptype extension icon32.png.
- Social image: existing Ziptype icon.png.
- Other illustrations are native HTML/CSS/SVG; no external images or icon CDNs.

Geist is used throughout. Geist Mono identifies literal shortcuts and values;
Geist Pixel appears only in decorative time counters. Fonts are served locally
with font-display: swap. No Google Fonts request or third-party script is needed.
